Every column targets the fall per quarter in the cost of fixed frontier performance. Staircase is the nonparametric check (pareto_frontiers.R, saved as staircase_check.html): record costs read off the empirical staircase at every grid node, over a one-year horizon, geometric-mean change compounded to a quarterly rate. BC surface is the SAME aggregate -- same nodes, horizon and compounding -- with the empirical record replaced by the Box-Cox Pareto-grid cost fit's surface, so the gap between the two columns is purely what smoothing does (levels of exactly 1 are excluded there; the node sets differ slightly). The remaining columns are the LINEAR fits' single coefficients: the cost-direction fits' time coefficient transformed directly, the accuracy-direction pair's -b_t/b_x ratio. SFA cost and OLS runs follow the model-effort run cloud rather than the record; negative values there are the cloud drifting dearer while the record collapses.
